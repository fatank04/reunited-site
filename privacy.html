Privacy policy

Effective August 9, 2026

The short version

Reunited collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics identifiers, and no Reunited servers. Everything the app knows lives on your phone.

Contacts

Reunited never requests access to your address book. When you add someone to your circle, you pick them one at a time through Apple's system contact picker, which hands Reunited only the people you tap. Those entries are stored on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Assessments and activity

Your check-in answers, streaks, and rep history are stored on your device. We cannot read them. Deleting the app deletes them.

AI features

Reply suggestions are generated by Apple's on-device foundation model. The messages you paste or share into Reunited are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to Reunited or any third party.

Plan something

When you search for a meetup spot, Reunited sends the activity and the town you typed (for example, "coffee shop in Montclair NJ") to Yelp or Apple Maps to get results. No names, contacts, or assessment answers are included in those requests.

The beta waitlist on this site

If you join the beta waitlist, the email or phone number you enter is delivered to us by Formspree, a form service, and lands in our support inbox. It is used to send your invite and for nothing else — no marketing list, and you can ask us to delete it any time. This is the one place anything on this website touches a third party; the app itself is unaffected.

Purchases

Hearth subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Reunited has no push servers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because Reunited has no accounts, we have no way to email you, which we consider a feature.

Contact

Questions: support@reunited.day, or see the support page.
