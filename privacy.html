Privacy policy

Effective August 9, 2026

The short version

Kin collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics identifiers, and no Kin servers. Everything the app knows lives on your phone.

Contacts

Kin never requests access to your address book. When you add someone to your circle, you pick them one at a time through Apple's system contact picker, which hands Kin only the people you tap. Those entries are stored on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Assessments and activity

Your check-in answers, streaks, and rep history are stored on your device. We cannot read them. Deleting the app deletes them.

AI features

Reply suggestions are generated by Apple's on-device foundation model. The messages you paste or share into Kin are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to Kin or any third party.

Plan something

When you search for a meetup spot, Kin sends the activity and the town you typed (for example, "coffee shop in Montclair NJ") to Yelp or Apple Maps to get results. No names, contacts, or assessment answers are included in those requests.

Purchases

Kin+ subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Kin has no push servers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because Kin has no accounts, we have no way to email you, which we consider a feature.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.
