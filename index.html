01

It names the person, not just the task.

"Reconnect with an old friend" still leaves the hard part to you. Reunited says who: whoever in your circle has drifted longest. Tap once and the message is drafted. You edit until it sounds like you, then send.

02

Every rep ends the same way.

Send the text, mark it done, and the screen says the only word that matters: Reunited. Two minutes, one person, and that feeling — every single day.

03

Your people, as embers.

Everyone you're keeping lights an ember. Send a rep and it glows. Let the weeks pass and it dims, gently. No guilt, no red badges, just the true state of your ties, made visible.

04

Stuck on a reply? It happens to everyone.

They wrote back. You froze. Share their message to Reunited and get three short ways to answer, written by AI that runs entirely on your iPhone. The message never leaves your phone, and the send is always yours.

05

Then take it off the screen.

Pick a person and a plan. Reunited finds a well-reviewed spot nearby and writes the invite: "Been too long. Coffee at Paper Plane this weekend?" A text keeps a tie alive. Being in the same room brings it back.

Today's rep

Private by architecture, not by promise.

Your contacts stay yours.

Reunited never asks for Contacts permission. You hand it individual people through Apple's picker; it cannot see the rest of your address book.

No account. No server.

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to breach. Your circle, streaks, and answers live on your phone and nowhere else.

AI that stays home.

Reply suggestions run on Apple's on-device model. The texts you're answering are never uploaded, to us or anyone.
